How this works and what it assumes
  • Claude turns your free text into structured jobs (power draw, duration, deadline, whether the job can pause) and writes the summary. Without Claude, a simple rule-based reader handles common phrasing.
  • The placement is a transparent greedy algorithm: biggest jobs first, lowest-carbon window before the deadline, never above the site power cap. Jobs that can pause are split across the cleanest hours.
  • Sunlight is a modeled clear-sky curve for the chosen city (from latitude, date and time), reduced by the sky setting. This page cannot fetch a live weather forecast.
  • Grid carbon intensity is assumed to fall from 450 to 120 g CO₂/kWh as solar output rises. Prices are illustrative time-of-use rates of $0.55 (4-9pm), $0.26 (9am-4pm) and $0.32 per kWh otherwise. Replace these with sourced figures before presenting.
  • The baseline runs each job at the earliest hours it fits. Total energy is the same in both schedules; only the timing changes.
  • One simulated site. Real deployments would plug into a job queue such as Kubernetes or Slurm and a grid carbon API.
